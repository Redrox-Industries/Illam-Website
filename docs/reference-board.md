## Visual language

- Warm ivory canvas with dark umber text and controls.
- High-contrast editorial serif headings paired with restrained sans-serif utility text.
- Large image-led scenes and broad negative space.
- Thin rules, quiet borders, pill-shaped actions, and minimal chrome.
- Asymmetric two-column compositions that become single-column on narrow screens.
- Tamil architectural character appears through material and spatial cues, not ornamental clutter.

## Interaction language

- Before/after comparison as a central proof point.
- Form controls use strong labels, simple underlines, and generous touch targets.
- FAQ rows use native disclosure behavior.
- Motion should reveal space gradually and remain optional under reduced-motion settings.

## Original direction for this build

Use a "quiet threshold" concept: visitors move from an exterior-like dark frame into warm, light interiors. Preserve the reference's calm pacing and editorial scale, but create original branding, copy, imagery, and layouts.
